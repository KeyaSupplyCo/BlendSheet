ℹ
How the buffer is calculated: Blend numbers are Ford vehicle sequence numbers shared across all lines. For each production line, we find the first part scanned closest to 6:00 AM (within a 4–8 AM window). You enter the customer's blend number at their 6:00 AM. Buffer = Our Blend − Customer Blend on that same line. Since all lines use the same vehicle sequence, a positive number means you are that many vehicles ahead of the customer. Negative means the customer is ahead of you — you need to produce more to catch up.
